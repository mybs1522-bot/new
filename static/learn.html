Before You Begin

All you need is three simple tools

No expensive machinery or special kit. You can find these at any local craft store or online for a few dollars.

1. A 4 mm or 5 mm Hook

Standard aluminum or ergonomic crochet hook. Sizes 4 mm (G/6) and 5 mm (H/8) are the most forgiving for learning proper yarn tension.

2. Medium-Weight Yarn

Worsted (Category 4) or 8-ply yarn. Pick a light pastel color (cream, mint, or pink) so you can easily see every individual stitch loop.

3. Scissors & Tapestry Needle

Any basic scissors to snip yarn ends, and a blunt large-eye tapestry needle to cleanly weave in your tails for a polished, seamless finish.
